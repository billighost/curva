The ritual

three steps. every night.

Ten minutes that belong only to you. Warm oil, slow circles, consistent practice.

01

warm & cleanse

Shower first. Pour four or five drops into your palm and rub them together until the oil stops feeling cold.

02

circular lift

Work upward in slow circles across hips, glutes and outer thighs. Keep going until the oil has gone and the skin feels warm.

03

nightly glow

Then leave it alone until morning. Repeat tomorrow. The consistency is the ritual — the results follow it, not the other way round.
